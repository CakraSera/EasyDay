# Pain gate

Pain or injury cues in the runner's Log (nyeri, sakit, pegal, ngilu, cedera,
painful, hurts, swollen, and similar) mean: no Quality, no running
intervals, Easy and Walk and Rest only. Never diagnose, never name a
condition, never explain anatomy. The week simply goes quiet on intensity.
Tired is not pain: lelah, capek, and sore from training are effort talk, not
a pain gate.
